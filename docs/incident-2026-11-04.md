# November 2026 incident and completed recovery

Production recovery completed on Arbitrum mainnet: all **9** approved transactions confirmed. The incident holder has zero vault shares, tickets 9–13 are cancelled without refund, both reserves and the bank lock are zero, and side bets remain disabled. Bank BRB, holder liquid BRB and BRB supply were preserved. Keeper approval and scheduler permissions were retained. Cloudflare automation stays paused.

See [the production execution record](incident-2026-11-04-production-script.md), `reports/incident-2026-11-04/mainnet-verification.json` and the timestamped mainnet transaction journal for receipt and state evidence.

## Current contract sources

`contracts/BankVault4626.sol` and `contracts/SideBet.sol` are the exact saved cleanup sources used for the final mainnet implementations. Their contract ABIs have no `recoverNovember2026` or `recoverNovember2026Tickets` entry points. Unused declarations/comments are retained to preserve the deployed compiler metadata.

The bank's pre-existing accounting and deposit/withdrawal behavior are unchanged. SideBet appends one boolean to its ERC-7201 storage struct. Its admin setter controls placement and both settlement endpoints; previews offer no work while disabled. The flag remains stored in the proxy across upgrades.

Temporary recovery implementations are preserved in immutable compiler inputs and `recovery-artifacts.json` under the incident report directory. They are used only for historical reproduction and tests, not as the current production sources. `before-recovery-deployment.json` freezes the old implementation addresses so updating the live manifest does not break the historical rehearsal.

## Reproduce on a local fork

From the contracts directory:

```sh
INCIDENT_FORK_BLOCK=521259858 node scripts/incident20261104-mainnet.cjs --fork
```

The original `incident20261104.cjs --fork` and `rehearseIncident20261104.cjs` commands delegate to that same fork runner. The pinned pre-recovery block is required now that mainnet is recovered; the execution preflight intentionally rejects an already recovered state.

The command compiles the current production sources, rebuilds archived recovery/cleanup artifacts with native Solidity 0.8.27, compares all four creation-bytecode hashes to the executed mainnet journal, validates namespace roots and recursive storage layouts, checks code-size limits, runs targeted tests, and executes the same 9-transaction sequence on local chain **31337**. Mainnet RPC access is read-only in fork mode. Native compiler discovery uses Hardhat's platform cache; `INCIDENT_SOLC` can select an explicit native 0.8.27 binary.

The recovery transaction burns only the fixed incident holder's current vault shares and calls the bank-only SideBet callback atomically. Cancellation failure rolls the burn back. Tickets 9–13 are cancelled without refund, and only their exact reserves are released; no global liquidity counter is reset directly. Both recovery functions are then removed through the cleanup upgrades.

## Validated properties

- Bank layout remains 14 fields. SideBet preserves the existing 11 fields and appends one boolean; roots, slots, offsets, types, mappings and array strides were compared through the original, recovery and cleanup versions.
- Recovery and cleanup deployments enforce runtime **24,576-byte** and initcode **49,152-byte** limits without a size bypass.
- Mainnet final-state readback confirmed original legitimate share supply, unchanged BRB balances/supply, preserved checked LP/other-bank positions and an unchanged withdrawal queue.
- Local tests cover failed callback rollback, unauthorized recovery/toggle calls, disabled side-bet endpoints, normal wagers/payouts and subsequent upgrades.
- The final cleanup bytecode was tested with 6- and 18-decimal assets through real scheduler/engine settlement, queued withdrawals and a subsequent deposit. These test operations are not part of the mainnet transaction sequence.

Keep side bets disabled until their accounting is separately redesigned and reviewed. This recovery does not claim that their old reservation accounting is safe to re-enable.

## drainWithdrawalQueue investigation

The deployed ordinary-roulette guard is unchanged. Tests use the real roulette engine with mock oracle delivery on the local EVM, covering 6- and 18-decimal assets:

- Ordinary stakes increase token balance and stake lock equally, leaving NAV/share price unchanged.
- Atomic `bet -> withdrawal request -> public drain` reverts while roulette needs the bank's capital; the bet, request, balances and locks roll back.
- The hatch remains blocked during pending/fulfilled VRF and partial payouts, even after `lockedBetLiquidity` reaches zero, until the market finishes settlement.
- An unrelated public caller can trigger payment only to the recorded withdrawal recipient, not to itself.
- A direct donation raises NAV and reduces the shares obtained by a fixed deposit; it does not produce this transaction's reserve-driven cheap-share mint.

The old side-bet reservation accounting did expose a discounted-price withdrawal path. This recovery clears the existing side-bet reserves and leaves the feature disabled; it does not claim that accounting is safe to re-enable.
